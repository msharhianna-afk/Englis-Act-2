MY UNFORGETTABLE SHS MEMORIES

My SHS memories are the most unforgettable experiences in my life.
We had so much fun creating different kinds of art, like drawing and paintings.
We enjoyed performing on the theater for our AD PROD and also performed our ONCE UPON A RHYME in ACT.
We designed the stage for one of our school events. Even though we were nervous and scared to perform, we encouraged and supported each other until we became confident.
Aside from our school activities, I also enjoyed the simple moments we shared together, like traveling, eating, working on projects, and studying together. We helped one another whenever someone had difficulty with a project or lesson, and these experiences made our friendship stronger.
